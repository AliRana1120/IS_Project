📚 Why bcrypt?
🧂 Automatic Salting
bcrypt generates a unique random salt per password, making rainbow table attacks impossible — even identical passwords produce different hashes.
⚙️ Work Factor
12 rounds of processing makes each hash computation expensive. As hardware improves, rounds can be increased without changing stored hashes.
🛡️ Brute Force Resistant
At 12 rounds, bcrypt takes ~250ms per hash — a billion attempts would take years, making offline brute-force attacks infeasible.
